1. CONTAINER & SURFACING:
   - Card Background: Solid pure white (#FFFFFF) with subtle drop-shadow (`shadow-2xl`).
   - Top & Bottom Edges: Jagged/zigzag tear-off SVG border pattern.
   - Font Family: Strictly Monospace / Courier (`font-mono`) for all text elements inside the receipt to mimic a dot-matrix printer.

2. HEADER & BRANDING:

     * "GOOD FOOD • GREAT VIBES" (Small, muted uppercase).

3. METADATA GRID:
   - Full-width horizontal divider line above and below.

     * Right Column: "Receipt No : FC[Timestamp]", "Payment Mode : UPI", "Transaction ID : [Generated Hash]".

   - Column Headers (Bold uppercase): "No. | ITEM | QTY | PRICE (₹)"
